# Achivii — Ideas

"Good to have" ideas that are **not problems**: nothing is broken without them. Problems and leftover work go in `docs/backlog.md` instead.

## How it works

- **Who adds ideas.** The reviewing chat, when a report lists ideas worth keeping (the report's "Ideas" section), or Mo at any time. Implementing chats do not edit this file.
- **Nobody works on an idea from this list.** Only Mo picks one. A picked idea moves: to a feature (a new folder under `docs/features/`, starting at `01-brief.md`, or a milestone of an existing feature) or to a backlog item, and its status says where.
- **Statuses.** `OPEN` · `MOVED to <feature or B-n>` · `DROPPED: <reason>`. Append new ideas at the bottom; never renumber. Pull the latest `main` and take the next number just before you write.
