## Objective

Make Journey immediately communicate the destination, current position, current week, and the current week’s role in the 12-week arc.

## Implementation

JourneyHeader now makes the first orientation answers explicit in the primary header: Destination, the user’s current Week/Phase position, and the current week’s focus/purpose. The existing journey visualization and strategic roadmap remain intact below it.

The change is deliberately additive and focused: no roadmap model, generated content, API, backend, or dashboard behavior was changed.

- **R5 No full scan required:** the four orientation answers are available before the detailed staircase/roadmap.
- **R6 Journey preserved:** phases, weeks, daily detail, closing stretch and destination remain rendered.
- **R7 Future honesty preserved:** existing future-week behavior and tests remain unchanged.
