### Goal
With `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED` on, someone back after a break longer than the plan can move chooses on Today between a fresh plan and keeping this one; "Keep going" jumps the weeks the calendar has passed and starts a whole first week back today; a plan whose dates have passed offers the final week. No week wholly in the past is ever written. A break shows no test card, and a high-load first week back has a light test. Targets do not change (RULE-18).

### Decisions I made
1. **The check answer's shape**: `{ due, weekNumber, testLogged, break, choice }`, `choice` null when none is needed. Other option: set `testLogged: true` for a break. Why: `testLogged` would then lie, and a break is a fact the app may need again.
2. **The 409 body**: `{ error, reason: 'choice_needed', choice }`. Other option: the plain `refuse` body. Why: the app and the review can tell which card to show without another check.
3. **A choice that is not the one needed** (`keep_going` sent when `final_week` is needed) is refused the same way. Other option: accept either. Why: the card the person saw decides; the server never picks for them.
4. **`writeNextWeek`'s `weekNumber` option** names the week to write; `finishedWeek` stays the closing week. Other option: pass `weekBack - 1` as `finishedWeek`. Why: last week's result, the handoff and `weekBefore` must read the week that closed, never an empty jumped week; without the option the call is exactly as before (R-2).
5. **Jumped weeks' tasks are deleted in the transaction** (they have none today). Other option: only update the rows. Why: makes "no tasks" hold even if a later change writes a week ahead; it deletes nothing today.
6. **The choice is held on every screen, the test card only on Today**; while a choice waits, reconcile and the fetch run as for the test card (goal fetched as stored). Other option: hold only on Today. Why: WU-38 (1).
7. **Which screens send to Today**: `/dashboard`, `/roadmap`, `/progress`, `/achievement`. Goal creation, billing return, legal and auth pages stay. Other option: every route but `/onboarding`. Why: the checkout return page and legal pages must keep working for a free user who goes for Pro from the fresh plan.
8. **A failed choice close** fetches the goal without reconcile and "Try again" sends the same choice. Other option: Try again runs the check and shows the card again. Why: the person already chose; reconciling a plan that is waiting for a choice would break RULE-17.
9. **The card is a `Surface` section, not a `Dialog`.** Other option: a `Dialog` like the test card. Why: nothing may dismiss it (dismissing means nothing here), and a dialog with no way out is worse for keyboard and screen-reader users.
10. **The time-trial rule** counts "time trial(s)", and "all-out" / "flat-out" / "as fast as you can/possible" only in a sentence with a distance (k, km, mi, miles, m, metres); a negation in the same clause makes it safe (the existing `negated`). Other option: any "all-out". Why: "all-out" alone is common in non-running drills; narrow, as the review asked.
11. **The light-test line also names the 80% of 1RM and 3 RIR limits**, since the light scan applies all weeks 1 to 3 rules; the block's heading says only the light test is checked. Other option: name only the test kinds. Why: the writer should be told every rule the week is checked against.
12. **Paused in the history list**: jumped weeks get a muted "Paused" entry with nothing else, and paused entries alone keep the empty state. Other option: leave them out of the history. Why: the requirement lists the history; a gap in the week numbers would be confusing.
13. **No phase milestone badge on a paused week**, even at a phase's last week. Why: no week was finished.
14. **Week 12 closing after a long gap keeps the test card** (no `break` for week 12: `planWeekMove` returns null). Other option: count a break for week 12 too. Why: RULE-11 says week 12 closing is unchanged and there is no next week to count a gap from; flagged below for the review.
