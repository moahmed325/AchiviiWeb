### Goal
With `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED` on, someone back after a break longer than the plan can move chooses on Today between a fresh plan and keeping this one; "Keep going" jumps the weeks the calendar has passed and starts a whole first week back today; a plan whose dates have passed offers the final week. No week wholly in the past is ever written. A break shows no test card, and a high-load first week back has a light test. Targets do not change (RULE-18).
