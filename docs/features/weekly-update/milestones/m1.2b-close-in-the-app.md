### Goal
With `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED` on, opening the app closes a finished week before anything else runs. Today asks for an unlogged test first, without ever blocking the app, shows "getting ready" while the week is written, and shows the close's line once. With it off, nothing in the app changes.

### Requirements
1. **Close before reconcile (RULE-17, WU-20 (4)).** Every goal load (`loadGoal`, `reloadPlan` and the new-date catch-up) calls `POST /close-week` first and runs reconcile only after it answers. `refreshGoal` stays fetch-only.
   - When the load is on Today (`window.location.pathname === '/'`), the load first uses check mode. If the close is due and the test isn't logged, the load holds the close: it fetches the goal without reconcile and Today shows only the test card (requirement 2).
   - On any other screen, or when the test is logged, the load closes at once.
   - A failed or 503 close never stops the goal from loading. Reconcile and the fetch run on the plan as stored, and Today shows the failure state (requirement 4).
2. **The test card (WU-14, WU-28).**
   - Copy, exact: "Did you do last week's test? Log your result to shape next week." Buttons: "Log result", "Skip".
   - "Log result" opens the test entry (reuse `ReviewTestResultStep`). Saving the result with `PUT …/test-result` then runs the close.
   - **Any other action counts as Skip and closes at once as unlogged:** "Skip", dismissing the card or the entry, or going to another screen (a route-change listener inside the router, e.g. in `AppShell`). Today stays a practice day.
   - Closing the browser tab does nothing: the card shows again on the next open.
   - After the close, reconcile runs, then the goal is fetched.
   - Skip and Log can't both run a close at the same time. The server is safe if they do, but the app sends one.
   - Use the `Dialog` and `Button` primitives; no new card or button.
3. **"Getting ready."** While the close call runs, the loading views show "Getting your next week ready…" (exact) instead of their usual loading state. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, no animation.
4. **A failed close.** After a 503, Today shows "Couldn't get your next week ready. Please try again." (exact) with a "Try again" button. The button runs the close again, then reconcile and the fetch. The rest of Today shows the plan as stored.
5. **The line once (WU-28).**
   - After an automatic close, Today shows the closed week's review insight once, as a dismissible line. Today it is always "Next week is ready. Your targets stay as planned."; later it will be the model's sentence.
   - Dismissing it records `closeLineSeenAt` through a small new endpoint, safe to repeat, for that goal's own closed week only, behind `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED`. It doesn't show again on any device.
   - Show it for a week with `closedBy: 'auto'` and no `closeLineSeenAt`. Add the new fields to the frontend `RoadmapWeek` type.
6. **A review that loses to an automatic close (WU-31).** A review modal can be open in a tab while another tab's load closes the week automatically. When its submit gets 409 `week_closed` and the week was closed automatically (`closedBy: 'auto'`, `reviewNotes` empty):
   - Save the typed reflection onto that week through a small new endpoint. It saves only on a week closed automatically with no reflection yet, behind `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED`, and is safe to repeat.
   - The modal then says "This week had already closed. Your reflection is saved." (exact).
   - In every other `week_closed` case, the M1.2a message and behaviour stay.
7. **Switch off (AC-19, R-7).** `POST /close-week` answers `{ due: false }`, and the load goes straight on to reconcile and the fetch. No card, no "getting ready", no line. Every existing e2e spec and test passes unchanged.
8. **Tests.**
   - Unit tests next to the components and the context, querying by role and name:
     - the load order (close, then reconcile, then fetch; check mode only on `/`);
     - the card: Log, Skip, dismiss, navigation; one close only;
     - "getting ready"; the 503 state and Try again; the line once;
     - the lost-race reflection; switch off.
   - Backend tests for the two small endpoints (switch off, the wrong week, a repeat).
   - An e2e spec with the close mocked in `e2e/mockApi.ts`, for the card, Skip, "getting ready" and the line, on desktop and mobile.
9. **Screenshots** in `milestones/m1.2b-evidence/`, desktop and mobile: the test card, "getting ready", the failure state, the line, and the modal's lost-race message.
