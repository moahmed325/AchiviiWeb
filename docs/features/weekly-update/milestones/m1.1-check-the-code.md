### 4. Missed sessions around an unwritten week (RULE-13, RULE-17, R-1, R-2)
Today, when the date is past the last written week:
- **Classification** covers stored rows only (`buildReconcileResult`, `missedSessions.ts:163-176`). Days after the last written week have no rows, so they are never missed, never planned, and nothing is carried into them. The closing week's open practice days are `missed` once their close has passed.
- **Gap:** the closing week's trailing missed days count (`findOpenGap` crosses weeks). The days after it count for nothing.
- **Signals** (`missedSignals.ts:206-221`): today has no row, so `today` is null. That means no gentle return, no swap offer, no short-on-time, and `planChoices.today` is null (`reconcileCore.ts:241`). Today keeps showing the old week with "Review due" (`Today.tsx:336,445`) and, after its test day, the late-test card.
- **Carry** (`recovery/carry.ts:292-296`): `todayWeek` falls back to the last written week, so the queued weeks are {last, last−1}. That week has no session left (`date >= today`), so its leftovers go `toNextWeek`.
- **Once the close writes the new week on its planned dates** (a 1-day gap, or any gap when the 28-day limit is used up), its rows dated before today become `missed` as soon as their close passes. They feed the gap, carries, the miss line and RULE-21. That is exactly what WU-13 forbids, so "not offered" must be stored.
  - **Recommended:** `DailyTask.notOffered Boolean @default(false)`, set when the close writes the week. Then `classifyDays` (`missedSessions.ts:111-116`) gives such a day a kind that is neither missed nor planned, for example a new `not_offered` kind, or `rest`.
  - **Cost of a new kind:** every consumer that switches on `kind` must handle it (carry, signals, `inOrderQueue`, `easyStart`, swap), so this is wide.
  - **Cost of reusing `rest`:** smallest. Rest days never count toward a gap and are never carried from. But `inOrderQueue` would then not count their in-order steps as not done, and WU-13 wants them queued into the days left. That needs checking in M1.4 (see Q3).
  - **Alternative:** don't write rows for those days at all, so they never exist. But the week writer always writes 7 days (`weekLayout`), and dropping rows breaks the 7-day week the screens expect.
- **R-2 (reconcile's week-before reading) with the close first.**
  - After the close, `todayWeek` is the new week and the queued weeks are {new, finished}. That is the same as after an on-time review followed by the next open.
  - One order difference I could not settle by reading: today, someone who reviews on day 8 gets reconcile at day 8's load **first**. With the queued weeks {finished, finished−1}, that may write `toNextWeek` records onto the finished week's rows. Then the review's handoff reads those rows. With the close first, the handoff reads the rows **before** reconcile writes them.
  - Whether `weekHandoff` gives the same result either way needs a test. M1.2's R-2 test should cover "review or close on the morning after the week ended", and not only an on-time review.
- **Reconcile never writes into a moved gap's days:** a moved plan has no rows for them, so this holds by itself. Days not offered need the classification change above. Jumped weeks have no rows if the close never writes them (recommended).

### 7. Safety for high-load goals (RULE-10, WU-25 (6))
- **Confirmed:** nothing limits plan v2 roadmap targets or a week's sessions for run10k or recomp.
  - `applySafetyClamps` is called only from `clampedTable` (`goal.ts:62-65`) on `Goal.velocityTable`, which no v2 goal has.
  - The roadmap's only safety gate is the model rating its own method (below 3 is refused, `ai/roadmap.ts:588-593`) plus `screenQuery` on the goal text.
  - In the week call, `isHighLoadGoal` (`lib/highLoad.ts:11-14`) only marks steps as high-load (`ai/weekPlan.ts:600`). That feeds RULE-14's rest gap; it sets no volume, deficit or intensity cap.
  - No running-volume, calorie or intensity numbers are checked anywhere in `taskRules.ts`, `scheduleRepair.ts` or `weekPlan.ts`.
- **A run10k goal after a 3-week break with dates only:**
  - The close writes the next week (say week 6) from its **stored week-6 target and test**, planned as if weeks 3 to 5 had gone to plan.
  - The writer is told "the weekly test was not done" and, with the method switch on, where the person is in each in-order kind.
  - It is told nothing about the break.
  - RULE-21 adds the easy line to one hard step only when the closing week ended with 3 or more missed practice days (see Corrections).
  - The person would be asked to run week 6's volume after 3 weeks off.
- **Smallest places for a limit that change no stored target:**
  1. **The week call's input** (`planV2.ts:177-206`): pass the first-week-back note from the return rule (section 6), plus "they have been away N days; keep every session at or below what they last completed". Told, not checked. This is the smallest change.

  3. **A pure post-write step like `easyStart`** (`recovery/easyStart.ts`): on the first week back of a high-load goal, the easy line on every hard step, or the 10-minute version as the default for the first N days. Deterministic and fails nothing.
  4. **Hold the close-only break for high-load goals until `WEEKLY_UPDATE_ENABLED`**: a break moves the dates but writes the week from the last reached week's target, told to the writer and not stored. This needs a product decision.
  - My recommendation for M1.5: 1 + 3. See Q6.

### Problems found
1. **The review route has no guard against repeats or old weeks** (`goal.ts:1083-1195`). A second submit, two tabs or a network retry runs the model again and **deletes and rewrites next week's tasks, losing progress on them**. A request for an older week moves `currentWeek` back. Two at once can leave duplicate rows. Its five writes are not one transaction. → `docs/backlog.md` (or M1.2a, Q8).
2. **Stale switch comments:** `recovery/switch.ts:3-4` and `backend/.env.example:58` say `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` "stays off until M3.3 is live (O2)", but it has been on in production since 2026-10-16. → `docs/backlog.md`, small.
3. **`journeyAdapter.ts` `mapTaskToStep`** compares the plan index (`task.dayNumber`) with the calendar day. That is fine today and wrong after any move unless "Day N" leaves out moved days. → covered by M1.3 or M1.4.
4. **`writeNextWeek` reads last week's result from the test day's notes**, not the logged `RoadmapWeek.testResult` (`planV2.ts:111-115`). A test logged in the review or late is never shown to the week writer. → `docs/backlog.md`, or M3.1.
