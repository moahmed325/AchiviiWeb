### Goal
After a break, the first week back restarts at the level the goal's return rule says, the targets left are spread from there, and the week starts with a re-test (AC-10, AC-18; RULE-16, WU-16, WU-18, WU-20 (2)).

### Decisions this prompt makes (WU-59, proposed by main - review)
1. **The restart level.** `n` is the closing week (the last week practised, also after "Keep going"). `back` is 0, 1 or 2 from the entry `returnRuleEntry` picks for `breakDays` (`last level`, `back 1 week`, `back 2 weeks`); no readable profile or no matching entry is `last level` (RULE-12). The restart level is the stored target of week `max(1, n − back)` as `safeGoalTargets` reads it, so week 1 is the floor (WU-20 (2)). It is never harder than week `n`'s target; if it is, or its kind or unit differs from week `n`'s, week `n`'s target is used and the reason is logged. On a light-test goal it is the target that week shows (a light week shows its next check), and it binds the week the model sets for next week (the big week next week shows, WU-49), with the light weeks before it showing it.
2. **Code checks it, the model spreads the rest.** On a break, the week the model sets for next week must equal the restart level exactly (a number within `EPSILON`, a deliverable's text exactly), checked like the `far_behind` hold is; the restart level is the lower bound of every week left (WU-16), replacing the last result; every other RULE-4 check still applies. The `far_behind`, unlogged and week-behind holds do not apply to next week on a break (the restart level is at or below this week's target, so it already holds). The model's status is not stored: the week a break started in has no status (WU-18, WU-53). No restart when next week shows week 12's target (WU-15, WU-47; this includes "Do my final week"); the update still runs then, as on any close of weeks 1 to 11 (RULE-1).
3. **The fallback keeps the restart.** A rejected, timed-out or failed break update still sets the week the model sets for next week (and the light weeks before it) to the restart level, with the other weeks as planned, through the same climb and storing path as an accepted one (as WU-48's holds). The plain `error` before the input is built and `unreadable_targets` stay the plain fallback.
4. **One re-test.** A break adds the re-test reason `break` (with `not_logged` too when the closing week's measured test was not logged); it is one re-test of the closing week's measured test, as M3.3a's. None after a light closing week (WU-56 (3)), when next week shows week 12's target, or with no offered practice day before the week's test day. In a first week back of a high-load goal (`goalLoad(...).highLoad`) the easy re-test (`EASY_RETEST_STEP`) is placed from the start, not the measured one: no time trial after weeks off (WU-41, WU-55), and it stores nothing (WU-56 (2) as amended). A measured re-test's result is week `n`'s late result; the latest wins, so it may replace a result logged before the break (WU-17).
5. **The app.** `retest.reason` gains `break`, chosen when the open week has `breakDays` (before `two_hard_weeks` and `not_logged`). Today shows no re-test line for it (the welcome-back line is already there; M4.1 owns any new copy); "Log re-test result" is offered as for the other reasons.
6. **The line.** An automatic close after a break stores its welcome-back line (`WELCOME_BACK_INSIGHT`, `KEEP_GOING_INSIGHT` or `FINAL_WEEK_INSIGHT`, as today), a space, then the update's sentence; when the update's line is the plain neutral line (`AUTO_CLOSE_INSIGHT`), only the welcome-back line (as WU-56 (4)). A review after a break stores the update's sentence, as any review.
7. **Jumped weeks.** The update's climb and read-back treat the weeks `jumpedWeeks` will jump as closed (`status: 'completed'`, `jumped: true`), so the update never stores a jumped week; the weeks left are `weekBack` to 12.

### Requirements
1. **The update runs on a break (RULE-1, RULE-16).** `updateRuns` no longer skips a break; a break that needs a choice is still refused with 409 `choice_needed` before any model call, as today. `planWeeklyUpdate` gets the close's `choice` and passes it to `planWeekMove`, so it knows `weekBack`. With either switch off nothing changes.
2. **Next week is `weekBack`.** The update's input names the week written (default `weekNumber + 1`), and `nextWeekIsLast`, `holdsOf`, `heldWeekOf`, the weeks-left guard and the prompt read it instead of `weekNumber + 1`. For a close that is not a break the prompt text, the checks and the stored result are exactly as today: add a test that a fixed non-break input builds the same prompt as on `8e9d5dd`.
3. **The restart level (WU-59 (1)).** One pure function, next to `returnRuleEntry`, giving `{ restart, level, week }` or null (no restart when next week shows week 12's target). Unit tests: each of the three restarts; no profile; a profile with no matching entry; a break in week 2 with `back 2 weeks` (week 1); a light-test goal closing in a light week and in a big week; a kind or unit change falling back to week `n`; "Keep going" counting from `n`.
4. **The check and the prompt (WU-59 (2)).** `checkWeeklyUpdate` refuses a break answer whose next-set week is not the restart level, with a reason naming the week and the level; the lower bound is the restart level; the hold checks are skipped on a break. Prompt 4 gets a short BREAK block only on a break: the days away, the return rule's restart in words, "Week {w} must be exactly {level}", and that the remaining weeks are spread from there. The model's status is not stored on a break.
5. **The fallback (WU-59 (3)).** As described. Tests: a time out and a twice-rejected answer on a break both store the restart level; on a light-test goal the light weeks before the big week show it.
6. **Jumped weeks (WU-59 (7)).** "Keep going" with jumped weeks: the weeks left are `weekBack` to 12, no jumped week is in `weeks` or `readBack`, and the climb starts as `safeStoredTargets` will read the goal after the save. Test with a jump over a big week (4 or 8) on a light-test goal.
7. **The re-test (WU-59 (4)).** `RetestReason` gains `break`; `retestOf` uses `weekBack`'s layout and dates, and on a high-load first week back asks the week call for the easy re-test from the start (an `easy` flag on `retest`, or the closest thing in `weekPlan.ts`); `retestOfWeekFor` then stores nothing. Tests: measured re-test after a break on a non-high-load goal stores `retestOfWeek = n`; easy on run10k stores nothing; none after a light closing week; `break,not_logged` gives one re-test.
8. **The line and the log (WU-59 (6)).** `autoCloseUpdateInsight` (or the break branch next to it) builds the line; tests for the three welcome-back lines with a sentence and with the neutral line. The `[WeeklyUpdate]` line adds `restart <last level|back 1 week|back 2 weeks|none>` after `status`; `retest` may now name `break`.
9. **RULE-6 (WU-18, WU-53).** A test that after a break the next close's two-hard-weeks check and `dailyTimeCheckOf` don't count the week the break started in (it has no stored status).
10. **The app (WU-59 (5)).** `retestInfo` gives `break` when the open week has `breakDays`; `RetestLineReason` and the frontend type gain it; `retestLine` returns null for it; the entry still shows. Unit tests both sides.
