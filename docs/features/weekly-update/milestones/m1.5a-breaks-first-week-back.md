### Goal
With `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED` on, someone back after a break of 7 days or more gets a first week back that the week writer shapes by the goal's return rule, a high-load goal's first week back is held down (WU-29), and Today says welcome back with the new end date. Targets do not change (RULE-18). The limit's choice card, jumped weeks and the fresh plan are M1.5b.

### Requirements
1. **A break (RULE-13, WU-33 (2)).** A close whose move has `gap` 7 or more is a break. With the switch off, or a gap under 7, nothing in this milestone acts. The move itself stays as M1.4 built it.
2. **The break's length (WU-18).** Days from the last day of the closing week with a done step (the closing week's first day if none was done) to the local date of the first open that tried to close the week, as `planWeekMove` dates it. Work it out once, before the model call. The length picks the return rule's entry: `any` for one rule; `1-2 weeks` under 21 days, `3+ weeks` at 21 or more. Store it as `breakDays` on the first week back's `RoadmapWeek`, in the close's transaction (a losing request saves nothing).
3. **The note to the week writer (WU-25 (3)).** For the first week back, the week call gets a block saying they have been away N days and the rule's `firstWeekBack` note. It says nothing about a target or a restart level (those are M3.4, behind `WEEKLY_UPDATE_ENABLED`). A goal with no readable profile gets only the "away N days" line. Told, not checked; it never fails the week.
4. **A high-load first week back (WU-29, WU-35).** For a high-load goal (`isHighLoadGoal`, or a profile with a `highLoad` kind):
   - the block adds "keep every session at or below what they last completed" (told, not checked);
   - after the call, code puts the first-week-back line first in every step of that week that is hard in the profile and high-load (a step of a high-load goal, or a step flagged `highLoad`), once. Exact text: "First week back: keep the effort easy today." Pure and deterministic; never fails the week; minutes unchanged; not on the 10-minute version.
   - A step that holds this line never also gets RULE-21's line, and a swap keeps both lines where they belong (`easyLineAfterSwap` must not strip or move the new line). Test both.
   - The stored target does not change.
5. **The line (section 6).** An automatic close that is a break saves "Welcome back. Your plan picks up from here and now ends on {date}." (exact), with `{date}` as written, instead of the moved or partial line. A review that closes after a break keeps the review's own insight and answer (as M1.4). Check the app fills `{date}` in it on Today and in the progress history.
6. **Switch off (R-7, AC-19).** With `WEEK_CLOSE_ENABLED` off: no break, no `breakDays`, no block, no line on any step, no new insight; the week call's prompt is exactly as before.
7. **Tests.**
   - Gaps of 6, 7 and 10 days: only 7 and 10 are breaks.
   - The length from the last done step: trained the first two days of the week then away 3 weeks gives `3+ weeks`; nothing done counts from the week's first day; a failed write retried later counts to the first try.
   - Rule choice for `any`, `1-2 weeks` and `3+ weeks` profiles, and no profile.
   - `breakDays` stored by the winner only.
   - The prompt block's text, with and without the high-load sentence, and the prompt unchanged without a break.
   - The first-week-back line: on every hard high-load step, not on easy or non-physical steps, not twice, not on a non-high-load goal; with RULE-21 and a swap.
   - The welcome-back line exact, on an automatic close only, `{date}` filled.
   - Switch off; R-2 (a normal week change with nobody away gives the same body).
   - e2e: a 10-day break shows the welcome-back line and today's step, desktop and mobile.
