### Stopped on
**Stop if (2): lowering on read can contradict a logged test result on a closed week.** For an older goal, a closed week whose test was logged against the stored target (say 8 km against 10 km, `passed: false`) now shows the lowered target (7 km) next to that stored "not passed" on Progress (`BenchmarkResultsCard.tsx:55`, `:136`) and Achievement (`AchievementResults.tsx:56-68`). The same is true, more mildly, for the open week: its days were written against the stored target, but the Roadmap and Today now show the lowered one. I built the milestone as written and did not change this; it needs a decision. Options:
- (a) Keep it as built: every week shows the lowered target. Simple, but a closed week can read "8 km, target 7 km, not passed".
- (b) **Recommended:** run the check on all weeks as now (so later weeks are still lowered from the safe climb), but send closed weeks (`status: 'completed'`, including jumped) with their stored target and text; lower only the open week and the weeks after it. The open week's test and the week call then use the safe target, which is the safety point; only past results keep the target they were judged against. A small change in `safeStoredTargets` (pass `currentWeek`) and one more test.
- (c) Also keep the open week as stored until it closes. Avoids the "days written for 10 km, target says 7 km" mismatch, but the open week's test would still ask for the too-steep number, so I would not choose it.
