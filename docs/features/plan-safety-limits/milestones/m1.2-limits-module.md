### Goal

### Requirements
1. **One limits module.** A new module (recommended `backend/src/lib/safety/limits.ts`) holds every number in `02-feature.md`'s table as named constants, including the four live ones. Move those out of `safetyClamps.ts`, and import them from the module in `safetyScan.ts`, `weekPlan.ts` and `safetyClamps.ts`, with no behaviour change. Leave the week-prompt literals ("at most 10% more", "2 hard runs") for M2.2. (RULE-1, AC-1)
2. **Target families.** A pure function reads a `WeekTarget` (plus whether the goal is run10k) and returns its family or none: running distance, running time, lift load, body weight, body fat, calorie deficit, as `02-feature.md` section 3 and PSL-10 say (pace is running time; continuous running minutes use the distance rule in minutes; a time or minutes target counts as running only on run10k or when the metric names a run; waist, weight lost, % of 1RM and RIR have no family). Deliverables have no family. (section 3, PSL-10)
3. **The next safe step.** An exported function `nextSafeStep(target, previousValue)` returns the furthest value the limits allow one week on, or null when the target has no family. It's what the weekly update will call (AC-11). Round toward the easier side, so a rounded value never breaks a limit. For a target in lb or miles, convert the allowance (+2.5 kg, +1 km) to that unit. (RULE-2, AC-11)
4. **Lowering in the roadmap check.** In `checkRoadmapAnswer`, after the unit is unified (`:691`) and before the stated-target check: check week 1 against the starting point, then each week against the one before. Lower any breaching target to the safe step, and re-check the following weeks from the lowered value. Never change a target that gets easier. A deficit target above 600 is lowered to 600, and one below 250 is left alone (PSL-6). With no starting-point value, skip week 1 and start from week 2 (`no_start`, PSL-11 Q14). This runs on the model's 12 targets, so the light weeks then copy the lowered values in `withPathwayTests` (PSL-11 Q1). (RULE-2, AC-2)
5. **Safety wins over the stated number.** When lowering alone puts a stated number out of reach, the stated-target check accepts week 12 at the highest safe step. A model answer that misses the stated number without being lowered is refused as today. Skip the `finalGoal` mention check when week 12 was lowered. (RULE-3, PSL-11 Q2, AC-3 server)
6. **Keep what the model wrote.** The accepted roadmap carries `loweredTargets: { week, model, safe }[]` (new goals only; missing means none), stored under `Goal.roadmap` by the existing `storedRoadmap` spread. `RoadmapWeek.target` keeps its shape and holds the safe value. (PSL-11 Q15, section 7)
7. **Logged once, with the goal id.** Goal create logs one `[Safety]` line per lowered target (rule `target_step`: week, model value, safe value, outcome `lowered`), plus one line each for `unknown_family` (a number target no family reads, logged once per goal) and `no_start`. Log only after the goal is saved, so the line has the goal id and a retried attempt never logs. Add the new rule names to `SafetyRule`. (RULE-10, AC-10)
8. **Widen the stated-target reader.** `extractStatedTargets` learns: two-letter units (kg, km, lb, mi) after a number; percent ("15% body fat"); "lose N <unit>", met when week 12 is at or below the starting point minus N on a body-weight target, or at or above N on a target that counts the amount lost (with no starting point, met; log nothing new); "in N minutes" or "in under N" as at most. "5 km in 25 minutes" gives one at-most time target, not an at-least one. "Run a 10K" and plan lengths stay non-targets. (PSL-5)
9. **Tests.** For each family: lowered at one step past the limit, left alone at the limit, in both directions where a direction applies. Also: the following weeks re-checked after a lowering; an easier week untouched; the deficit over 600 and under 250; unknown family and `no_start` logged, never refused; a run10k split roadmap whose light weeks hold the lowered big-week values; a stated number out of reach after lowering accepted, while a plain miss is still refused; `finalGoal` not asked for again when week 12 was lowered; one log line per lowered target after create, none from a retried attempt; `nextSafeStep` for each family and for a target with no family; every phrase in requirement 8, plus each existing `statedTarget.test.ts` case unchanged.
