## Are the limits holding back a safe amount? (Mo's question)
No limit read the person's level, so a beginner and someone already training got the same caps. Each limit against common coaching guidance:

| Limit | Verdict | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Running total +10% (or +10 min / +1 km) on the base week | fine: builds on what they did | kept |
| Longest run 50% of the week (3+ runs) | generous (coaches say 25 to 35%) | kept |
| Hard runs at most 2 | standard | kept |
| Race time at most 2% faster a week | about 20% over 12 weeks | kept |
| Body weight down at most 1% a week; body fat 0.5 points | top of normal guidance | kept |
| Intake never below 1,200 kcal | a floor | kept |
| First week back no harder than before the break | standard | kept |
| **Longest run with no base, fewer than 3 runs: 30 min / 5 km** | held experienced runners to a 5 km long run in week 1 | **PSL-19**: never below what they already run, from their starting point |
| **Weeks 1 to 3 lifting: 80% of 1RM, 3 RIR** | three easy weeks for experienced lifters; overrode recomp's own 1 to 3 RIR | **PSL-20**: beginners only; failure and max tests still wait |
| Deficit at most 600 kcal | low for a heavy person | **PSL-22**: kept (not a medical app) |
| Lift target +5% or 2.5 kg a week | slow for beginners, safe | **PSL-22**: kept |
