## Prompt

### Context

For untagged steps, `isHighLoadStep(step, goal)` in `backend/src/lib/highLoad.ts` is today's test (a goal-level high-load goal such as run10k or recomp makes every step high-load).

- Tests: `backend/test/methodCarry.test.ts` (block "MR-27 until M3.1b"), `methodReconcile.test.ts`, the shared profile `methodProfile.ts`.

### Goal
With the switch on, a moved step keeps its kind's order (shifting the later steps of that kind) and never lands closer to another hard step than the rest gap. Generated weeks prove that no day gets longer and no rule is broken. With the switch off, everything is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **Remove MR-27's drops.** `hard_waits` and `out_of_order` go away (from `DropReason` too), and so do the "MR-27 until M3.1b" tests, replaced by the tests below. MR-27's continue part (no later session with the kind is no room) stays.
2. **Rest gap (RULE-14).** A moved hard step may land only where no other hard step is within `restGapDays` full calendar days of it, looking at every day of the goal, last week included. Count hard steps on days that are done, today or later; a missed day's steps did not happen and do not count; the step's own missed day does not count. Hard means `actionOf(...).hard` for a step with a known kind, and `isHighLoadStep` for a step without one. A carried hard step never shares a day with a hard step that stays on that day. A day that fails is skipped and the search goes on (RULE-9: first later day that passes).
3. **Order (RULE-13).** An in-order step may land on a day before the next step of its kind (an ordinary fit), or on the day of that next step, where it takes that step's place. It may never land after it. When it takes the place, that step moves to the next session this week with a step of the kind and takes its place, and so on; the last one pushed past the week is not done this week. Each shifted step must fit its new day: the day never gets longer, a fixed or continue-marked step is never replaced (MR-17), the day is not done, closed (missed or before today) or already holding a carry, and the rest gap holds for it. If any link fails, nothing in the shift happens and the missed day is no room (MR-18: the rest gap wins).
4. **Markers.** Each shifted step gets a stored marker (for example `shiftedFrom: { taskId, date }`), in the step JSON, no schema change. A shifted step is never carried or shifted again, and a later run never redoes a shift (ND-13, the same as `carriedFrom`). MR-26's warm-up line goes only on the step that was missed, not on shifted ones.
5. **One transaction.** A shift writes every changed day in one `prisma.$transaction`, each day guarded as `writeRecovery` guards it today. If any guard fails, the whole shift is rolled back and nothing of it is written. An ordinary carry or continue keeps its single guarded write.
6. **Counts and body.** The missed day's outcome stays `moved` (RULE-15). The step pushed past the week adds one to a new count, `weekCounts.pushedOut`, present only when the plan has `outcomes` (as `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom` are); `noRoom` stays one per missed day. Nothing tells next week's writer about the pushed-out step yet (`writeNextWeek` does not read `weekCounts`), so for now it is lost: do not add that here (M3.2 owns it, MR-29). With the switch on, the body adds `carry.shifts` (each shifted step: from, to, kind, title). Every existing field stays as it is.
7. **Generated-week tests (AC-6).** At least a few hundred generated weeks with tagged steps (random kinds from a profile with in-order and hard kinds, rest gaps 0 to 2, statuses, today, last week's hard days) run through `planRecovery` and check after every plan: no day longer than written, no fixed or continue-marked step replaced, no two hard steps closer than the gap, no two hard steps sharing a day that did not as written, every in-order kind still in order, no step carried or shifted twice, nothing on the test day or a rest day. Include a run10k or recomp week written before kinds: its untagged steps still drop as `high_load` under missed sessions' rules (RULE-18).
8. **Other tests.** The interval-run row of section 5 (not moved onto the day before the long run, the next day that passes is used, or no room); the lesson row (Lesson 3 takes Lesson 4's place, Lesson 4 moves on, the last one is pushed out and counted); a shift that fails on one link changes nothing; the rest gap reaching into last week; the transaction rolls back when one guard fails (route test with a mocked `$transaction`); a second run plans no new shift; switch off, no profile, or untagged steps give exactly today's plan and body (the M3.1a checks still pass).

### Summary
`planRecovery` (`backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`) now keeps the rest gap and the order when the method switch is on. MR-27's `hard_waits` and `out_of_order` drops are gone. With the switch off, `planRecovery` still returns `planCarries(input)` itself, and missed sessions' planner is unchanged.

- **The move search (RULE-9, RULE-14).** It walks the later practice days of the week, as before. For a hard step (by `actionOf`, or `isHighLoadStep` for a step without a known kind), a day where the step fits is skipped when any hard step sits within `restGapDays` calendar days of it. That includes a hard step on the same day that the fit leaves in place. The search then goes on to the next day. All days of the goal count (last week too) when they are done or still open. A missed day does not count, and that includes the step's own missed day.
- **Order (RULE-13).** For an in-order step, the search stops at the first later session that holds a step of its kind (the "next step"). A day before that gets an ordinary fit. On the next step's day, the moved step takes that step's place (`takePlace`), the next step takes the place of the one after it, and so on through every session of the kind this week. The last one is pushed past the week. Each link must hold, or nothing happens and the day is `shift_blocked` (no room). A link holds when: the day is open, not done and not taken; it never gets longer; nothing fixed, continue-marked or already moved is replaced; and the rest gap holds for every step that moves (MR-18).
- **Markers (ND-13).** Each shifted step gets `shiftedFrom: { taskId, date, replaced }`. The missed step's `carriedFrom` gets `pushedOut` (the pushed-out step as written). A step with `shiftedFrom` is never carried or shifted again: it is left behind on its day like a carried step, it can never be a link, and a day holding one is taken. MR-26's warm-up line goes only on the missed step.
- **Many missed days in one run.** They are planned most recent first, so ND-12 (most recent wins) is unchanged. The rest gap also sees the carries and shifts this run has already accepted, so one run gives the same result as a replan after each write.
- **Writes (requirement 5).** `writeRecovery` writes a shift's receiving day and every shifted day in one `prisma.$transaction`, each with the same guard as today. A failed guard throws inside the transaction, so it rolls back, and nothing of the shift is in `written`. An ordinary carry or continue keeps its single guarded write.
- **Counts and body.** `weekCounts.pushedOut` (present with `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom`) is one per shift, counted from the planned shift or, once written, from the stored `carriedFrom.pushedOut`. `carry.shifts` (switch on only) lists each shift: the missed day, the kind, the receiving day, every moved step (`fromTaskId`, `fromDate`, `toTaskId`, `toDate`, `kind`, `stepTitle`; `to` is null for the pushed-out step) and the shifted days' rows. The missed day's outcome stays `moved`.

### Decisions I made
| Choice | Other option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hard steps count on days that are **done or still open** (`planned`), whatever their date. | Only done days, today and later. | A day dated yesterday that has not closed yet (before its sleep-time close) can still be done, so it is safer to count it. In practice it is the same set. |
| A day that fails the rest gap is skipped **before** the closed/done/taken checks, so the search can pass a closed day and land later. | Stop at the first day where the step fits, then apply the gap. | RULE-9 and MR-10 say "the first later day that passes RULE-12 to RULE-14"; a day that fails RULE-14 does not pass. |
| Missed days are planned most recent first; this run's accepted carries and shifts count for the rest gap and for "taken". The fit itself still reads the rows as written plus stored and this run's continue markers (as M3.1a). | Check the gap only against the week as written. | Two hard carries in one run could otherwise land closer than the gap, or one run could say "no room" while the replan after the write moves the step. ND-12 still holds exactly: an older day that reaches a day a later one reached is `lost_to_later_miss`. |
| On the day being tried, the gap also counts this run's carry onto it and any stored carried or shifted step, even if the fit would trim them. | Use only the fitted row. | Otherwise a replan could trim a stored carried hard step and accept the day (it is then taken anyway), giving a different result than the first run. |
| `takePlace`: the step keeps the replaced step's position and priority. If it is longer, it trims the day's lowest-priority steps, but never priority 1, fixed, continue-marked, carried or shifted, untagged steps, or steps of the same in-order kind. | Use `fitCarriedStep` (always placed after the day's own priority-1 step). | "Takes that step's place" means its slot; trimming the same kind would lose a lesson out of order. |
| The step whose place is taken on a day is that day's highest-priority step of the kind. A link fails if that step is fixed, continue-marked, or already carried or shifted. | Pick by position. | Matches how continue picks its step (M3.1a). Moving an already-moved step would break ND-13. |
| The pushed-out step is stored on the missed step's `carriedFrom.pushedOut` (the full step as written). | On the last shifted step's marker. | When the next step is the last of the week there is no shifted step to hold it. The carry always exists, so the count survives the write, and M3.2 can read which step it was. |
| `shiftedFrom` holds `replaced` too (the step it replaced plus any trimmed steps). | Only `{ taskId, date }`. | Same audit trail as `carriedFrom` (ND-10, ND-13); nothing trimmed is lost without a record. |
| One new drop reason, `shift_blocked`, for any failed link, including the rest gap. Search with no day that passes stays `no_receiving_day`. | A separate `rest_gap` reason. | Both count as no room; the prompt asks for no more detail. |
| `carry.shifts` is always present (maybe `[]`) when the method switch is on, like `continues`. There is no `writtenShifts`: a shift is written exactly when its carry is in `written`. | Add `writtenShifts`. | Keeps the body small; the transaction makes the carry and the shift one unit. |
| A missed-sessions carry whose step holds `shiftedFrom` is turned into a `no_priority_step` drop inside `planRecovery`. | Teach `planCarries` about the shift marker. | The prompt says to stop if `planCarries` must change. Today a shifted step can only reach missed sessions' rules on a day with no tagged step left, which a one-call week does not produce; this keeps "never carried again" anyway. |
| A day holding a stored `shiftedFrom` step is taken: it receives no carry and is no shift link. | Let it receive. | One move per day per run, as with `carriedFrom`; simplest guarantee that nothing is moved twice. |
| `pushedOut` is counted in the missed day's week. | The pushed-out step's week. | The same key as the other counts; both are the same week. |
| (Round 1.) Carried steps (`carriedFrom`) are **not** added to the ordinary fit's protection; only swapped and in-order steps are. | Protect every marked step in the fit. | A day holding a carry is already taken. Protecting it would let the search skip past a taken day instead of stopping there, which would change M3.1a's "stops at a day that already holds a carry". |
