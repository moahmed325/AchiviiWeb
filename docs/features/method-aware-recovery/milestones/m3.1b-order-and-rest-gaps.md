## Prompt

### Context
M3.1a made the carry planner follow each step's kind behind `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` (off in production; the carry switch `MISSED_SESSIONS_CARRY_ENABLED` is **on**). Two parts were left for this milestone and are held back today by MR-27. Verify these facts; do not trust this prompt.

- Planner: `planRecovery` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`. The move block drops a hard mover as `hard_waits` and an in-order mover whose receiving day is at or after the next step of its kind as `out_of_order` (both declared in `DropReason` in `backend/src/lib/carryForward.ts`). The receiving-day search is `laterInWeek(missed)` with `fitCarriedStep(..., isProtected)`; ND-12 (most recent wins) and the done, closed and taken checks come after the search.
- Profile: `RecoveryProfile.restGapDays` (full days between two hard steps, 0 to 2) and `RecoveryKind.inOrder` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/profile.ts`. `actionOf(step, profile)` returns `{ action, hard }`; a `highLoad` step is hard. For untagged steps, `isHighLoadStep(step, goal)` in `backend/src/lib/highLoad.ts` is today's test (a goal-level high-load goal such as run10k or recomp makes every step high-load).
- Load: `RECONCILE_GOAL_SELECT` in `backend/src/lib/reconcileCore.ts` loads **every** `DailyTask` of the goal, so last week's days are in the input.
- Writes: `writeRecovery` in `reconcileCore.ts`, one guarded `updateMany` per receiving day (ND-13), only when `carryEnabled()`. A one-transaction write already exists in `lib/planV2.ts` (`prisma.$transaction(async (tx) => ...)`).
- Tests: `backend/test/methodCarry.test.ts` (block "MR-27 until M3.1b"), `methodReconcile.test.ts`, the shared profile `methodProfile.ts`.

### Goal
With the switch on, a moved step keeps its kind's order (shifting the later steps of that kind) and never lands closer to another hard step than the rest gap. Generated weeks prove that no day gets longer and no rule is broken. With the switch off, everything is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **Remove MR-27's drops.** `hard_waits` and `out_of_order` go away (from `DropReason` too), and so do the "MR-27 until M3.1b" tests, replaced by the tests below. MR-27's continue part (no later session with the kind is no room) stays.
2. **Rest gap (RULE-14).** A moved hard step may land only where no other hard step is within `restGapDays` full calendar days of it, looking at every day of the goal, last week included. Count hard steps on days that are done, today or later; a missed day's steps did not happen and do not count; the step's own missed day does not count. Hard means `actionOf(...).hard` for a step with a known kind, and `isHighLoadStep` for a step without one. A carried hard step never shares a day with a hard step that stays on that day. A day that fails is skipped and the search goes on (RULE-9: first later day that passes).
3. **Order (RULE-13).** An in-order step may land on a day before the next step of its kind (an ordinary fit), or on the day of that next step, where it takes that step's place. It may never land after it. When it takes the place, that step moves to the next session this week with a step of the kind and takes its place, and so on; the last one pushed past the week is not done this week. Each shifted step must fit its new day: the day never gets longer, a fixed or continue-marked step is never replaced (MR-17), the day is not done, closed (missed or before today) or already holding a carry, and the rest gap holds for it. If any link fails, nothing in the shift happens and the missed day is no room (MR-18: the rest gap wins).
4. **Markers.** Each shifted step gets a stored marker (for example `shiftedFrom: { taskId, date }`), in the step JSON, no schema change. A shifted step is never carried or shifted again, and a later run never redoes a shift (ND-13, the same as `carriedFrom`). MR-26's warm-up line goes only on the step that was missed, not on shifted ones.
5. **One transaction.** A shift writes every changed day in one `prisma.$transaction`, each day guarded as `writeRecovery` guards it today. If any guard fails, the whole shift is rolled back and nothing of it is written. An ordinary carry or continue keeps its single guarded write.
6. **Counts and body.** The missed day's outcome stays `moved` (RULE-15). The step pushed past the week adds one to a new count, `weekCounts.pushedOut`, present only when the plan has `outcomes` (as `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom` are); `noRoom` stays one per missed day. Nothing tells next week's writer about the pushed-out step yet (`writeNextWeek` does not read `weekCounts`), so for now it is lost: do not add that here (M3.2 owns it, MR-29). With the switch on, the body adds `carry.shifts` (each shifted step: from, to, kind, title). Every existing field stays as it is.
7. **Generated-week tests (AC-6).** At least a few hundred generated weeks with tagged steps (random kinds from a profile with in-order and hard kinds, rest gaps 0 to 2, statuses, today, last week's hard days) run through `planRecovery` and check after every plan: no day longer than written, no fixed or continue-marked step replaced, no two hard steps closer than the gap, no two hard steps sharing a day that did not as written, every in-order kind still in order, no step carried or shifted twice, nothing on the test day or a rest day. Include a run10k or recomp week written before kinds: its untagged steps still drop as `high_load` under missed sessions' rules (RULE-18).
8. **Other tests.** The interval-run row of section 5 (not moved onto the day before the long run, the next day that passes is used, or no room); the lesson row (Lesson 3 takes Lesson 4's place, Lesson 4 moves on, the last one is pushed out and counted); a shift that fails on one link changes nothing; the rest gap reaching into last week; the transaction rolls back when one guard fails (route test with a mocked `$transaction`); a second run plans no new shift; switch off, no profile, or untagged steps give exactly today's plan and body (the M3.1a checks still pass).
