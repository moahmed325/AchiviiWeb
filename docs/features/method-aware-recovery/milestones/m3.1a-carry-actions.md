## Prompt

### Context
Every new step now carries a kind from the goal's profile (M2.1, M2.3). Nothing acts on it yet. This milestone makes the carry planner follow each step's action, behind a new switch that stays off. Verify these facts; do not trust this prompt.

- Carry planner: `planCarries` in `backend/src/lib/carryForward.ts` (pure). Today it takes each missed day's priority-1 step (`lead`), finds one fixed receiving day (ND-17: the first later practice day of the same week, not rest, not the test day), lets the most recent missed day win (ND-12), **drops a high-load lead** (`isHighLoadStep` → `high_load`), holds a key session for a swap answer (ND-9), and fits the step with `fitCarriedStep` (replaces the receiving day's lowest-priority steps; the day never gets longer). Stored markers: `carriedFrom` (`CarryMarker`) and `swappedFrom`.
- Writes: `writeCarries` and `runReconcile` in `backend/src/lib/reconcileCore.ts`; guarded `updateMany` writes, only when `carryEnabled()` (`MISSED_SESSIONS_CARRY_ENABLED` exactly `true`). Body type `ReconcileBody`. **The carry switch is on in production**, so with the new switch off, real users get exactly today's carries: requirement 2 matters.
- Counts: `weekCounts` in `backend/src/lib/missedSignals.ts`. Signals: `buildSignals` (same file).
- Profile helpers: `readRecoveryProfile(roadmap, facts)` and `actionOf(step, profile)` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/profile.ts`. `actionOf` returns null without a known kind, `fixed` for the test step, and treats a `highLoad` step as hard and a high-load continue as move (MR-11).
- Completion is per day (`DailyTask.status`); steps have no done flag; the product has no streak.
- The switch test for the carry switch is in `backend/test/reconcileCarry.test.ts` ("render.yaml (ND-15)").

### Goal
With `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` on, a missed day's steps follow their kinds' actions (move, continue, let go, fixed), and the week counts say what happened. With it off, or for a step without a kind, everything is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **The switch.** `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED`, on only when exactly `true`, read per request, off by default. Declared in `render.yaml` with `sync: false` and a comment that it stays off until M3.3 is live, when Mo switches it on in the Render dashboard (O2); a yaml test like ND-15's. Listed in `backend/.env.example`, described in `CLAUDE.md`. Never turned on from code or tests. Carry writes still need `MISSED_SESSIONS_CARRY_ENABLED` as well.
2. **When the new rules apply.** Only when the switch is on, the goal has a valid profile, and the missed day's steps have known kinds. Otherwise that day goes through today's planner unchanged (RULE-18, MR-6). Today's other steps stay as they are: the open gap, already-handled days, receiving days before today counted as closed, and the key-session hold (ND-9; M3.2 owns swaps).
3. **Move (RULE-9, MR-10).** The day's highest-priority step whose action is move moves. Its receiving day is the first later practice day of the same week, not the test day, where the fit succeeds without replacing a fixed step (RULE-12, MR-17) or a step holding a continue marker. If that day is done, closed or already holds a carry, or no day passes, the step goes to no room. When two missed days get the same receiving day, the most recent wins (ND-12). Until M3.1b adds the rest gap and the order shift (MR-27): a hard step goes to no room, and an in-order step goes to no room unless its receiving day comes before the next step of its kind.
4. **MR-26.** Every moved step gets exactly "Start with an easy 5-minute warm-up and end with 5 easy minutes." as the first line of its instructions. Added once only (not again if it moves again); the step's minutes do not change. The steps left behind are not moved and not counted.
5. **Continue (RULE-10).** Nothing moves. For each continue kind on the missed day, the next session this week with a step of that kind gets a stored marker on that step (for example `continueFrom: { taskId, date }`), written with the same guard as a carry, once. A carry never replaces a step holding one. If there is no later session with that kind, it counts as no room (MR-27).
6. **Let go and fixed (RULE-11, RULE-12).** Nothing moves and nothing is added. A fixed step is never moved by a carry, and a carry never replaces it.
7. **Counts (RULE-17, MR-19, MR-26).** `weekCounts` adds `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom`, one per missed day, by the outcome of its highest-priority step. The steps MR-26 leaves behind add nothing. `practiceDone` and the other counts do not change. No screen shows the new counts.
8. **The reconcile body says what ran.** Each missed day in the body says which rules it followed (`method` or `missed_sessions`) and its outcome (`moved`, `continued`, `let_go`, `fixed` or `no_room`), so M3.3 can choose its line (RULE-15). The existing fields stay as they are.
9. **Tests.** Each action with its own test (AC-5); the receiving day search (skips a day whose only room is a fixed or continue-marked step, stops at a done, closed or taken day); most recent wins; the temporary hard and in-order rules; MR-26 (the line on every moved step, added once, minutes unchanged); the continue marker written once and never replaced by a carry; counts, with the left-behind steps not counted and `practiceDone` unchanged; **switch off, no profile and steps without kinds give exactly today's plan and body** (run the existing carry tests' inputs both ways); nothing is written while `MISSED_SESSIONS_CARRY_ENABLED` is off; the yaml test.

### Summary
A new pure planner, `planRecovery` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`, wraps missed sessions' `planCarries`. Without the `method` input (the switch is off) it returns `planCarries(input)` itself, so the plan and the reconcile body are byte-for-byte today's. With `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` exactly `true`, `evaluate` reads the profile from `Goal.roadmap.recovery` (`readRecoveryProfile`) and passes it in. Then:

- `planCarries` still runs on the same input. Every day that does not qualify keeps exactly its result (requirement 2). A day qualifies when it is missed, not handled, the profile is valid, every step has a known kind (`actionOf` not null) and a priority, and it has at least one step of its own. The open-gap drops and the key-session holds of qualifying days are kept too.
- A step carried onto a day (`carriedFrom`) is never carried again, as in `planCarries` (ND-13). It is treated as left behind: it is not the day's move step, its top step or a continue source, and it adds to no count. A day holding only carried steps stays on missed sessions' rules.
- Days of the open gap get the outcome `in_gap` (and `topStep: 'in_gap'`) under either rules, unless their carry was already stored (`moved`).
- **Continue** (RULE-10) is planned first. For each continue kind on the day, it finds the next session this week (not rest, not the test day) with a step of that kind, and stages `continueFrom: { taskId, date }` on that step. The step is the lowest-priority-number one of that kind. If that session is done or closed, the step already holds a marker, or no such session exists, the kind is no room. If two days reach the same step, the most recent wins.
- **Move** (RULE-9, MR-10, MR-17) takes the day's highest-priority move step:
  - It is hard (by `actionOf`, so high-load counts): `hard_waits`, no room (MR-27).
  - Otherwise the search walks the later practice days of the same week (not the test day). It stops at the first day where `fitCarriedStep` succeeds without replacing a fixed step or a step holding a continue marker (new optional `isProtected` argument).
  - Nothing passes: `no_receiving_day`.
  - The step's kind is in order and a step of that kind sits on any day after the missed day up to and including the receiving day: `out_of_order` (MR-27).
  - ND-12, most recent wins: `lost_to_later_miss`.
  - The receiving day is done: `receiving_day_done`. It was missed or is dated before today: `receiving_day_closed`. It already holds a carry (stored, or one missed sessions makes in this run): `receiving_day_taken`.
  - Otherwise the step is carried with MR-26's line first in its instructions, added once, with the same minutes.
- **Let go and fixed**: nothing is moved and nothing is added. A fixed step is protected from replacement and is never the moved step.
- **Writes**: `writeRecovery` makes one guarded `updateMany` per receiving day. A carry and a continue marker that land on the same day share that row. Writes still happen only when `carryEnabled()`.
- **Body**: only when the method switch is on, `carry` adds `outcomes` (per missed day: `rules`, `outcome` by MR-12, and `topStep`), `continues`, `alreadyContinued` and `writtenContinues`.
- **Counts**: when `outcomes` is given, `weekCounts` adds `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom`, all from the planned `topStep` of days under the new rules (gap days are `in_gap`, so in none).
- **Load**: `RECONCILE_GOAL_SELECT` is as in M2.2; `loadReconcileGoal` adds `roadmap: true` only when the switch is on.
