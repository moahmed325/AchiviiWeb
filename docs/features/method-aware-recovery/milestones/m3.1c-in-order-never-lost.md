## Prompt

### Context
M3.1b (merged as `27133e1`) moves one missed in-order step by a one-step shift. MR-30 replaces that with one rule: an in-order step is never lost. MR-31 adds an easy line after a break and limits the warm-up line to physical steps. Everything stays behind `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` (off in production; the carry switch is **on**). Verify these facts; do not trust this prompt.

- Planner: `planRecovery` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`. The shift is `planShift` with `takePlace`; the rest gap is `restGapHolds` (MR-29 (2) and (5)); protection is `isKept` / `isProtected` (MR-29 (4)); drop reason `shift_blocked`; `PlannedShift` in the plan and `carry.shifts` in the body; `carriedFrom.pushedOut` on the carried step and `weekCounts.pushedOut` in `backend/src/lib/missedSignals.ts`.
- Steps are left behind today in three places: `methodSteps` skips any step holding `carriedFrom` or `shiftedFrom` (MR-28 (2)); only the day's top move step moves (RULE-9, MR-26); open-gap days get `in_gap` and nothing moves (ND-11, `findOpenGap` in `backend/src/lib/missedSessions.ts`).
- Writes: `writeRecovery` in `backend/src/lib/reconcileCore.ts`, one guarded write per day, and one `prisma.$transaction` for a shift (all or nothing).
- Warm-up line: `MOVED_WARM_UP_LINE` and `withWarmUpLine` in `carry.ts`, added to every moved step. Profiles carry `template` (`endurance`, `strength`, ...); the run10k and recomp pathways use the endurance and strength templates (`recovery/pathways.ts`).
- Tests: `backend/test/methodCarry.test.ts`, `methodOrderGap.test.ts` (the generated weeks), `methodReconcile.test.ts`.

### Goal
With the switch on, every step of an in-order move kind is either done, held by a later session this week in the right order, or listed as not done for next week. After a break, the first hard session back is marked easy. With the switch off, everything is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **The queue (RULE-20, MR-30 (1)).** For each in-order kind whose action is move, a pure function works out the steps of that kind not done yet, in order: first those on days of this week that closed undone (missed days, days of a break, a lower-priority step, a step that was carried or shifted there and missed again), then the ones on the remaining sessions as written. Export it, because M3.1d will use the same function at the week's end to tell next week (do not wire it into `writeNextWeek` here). It is worked out from the stored week (steps, markers, day status), so a replan with nothing new gives the same result.
2. **Reorder inside the week.** The week's remaining sessions of that kind (today or later, not done, not closed, not the test day) keep their days and take the queue's steps in order. Each placed step must fit its day: the day never gets longer, a fixed, continue-marked, swapped or in-order step of another kind is never trimmed (MR-29 (4)), and the rest gap holds as MR-29 (2) and (5) say. If a session can't take the next step safely, it gives up its own step of the kind (that step joins the queue in its place, order kept) and the next session is tried (MR-30 (5): safety first, then order, then keep going). The steps left over are not done this week (requirement 5). Each step that changed session stores a marker inside `detailedSteps` (keep `shiftedFrom` or rename it; say which). Every changed day of one reorder is written in one transaction, all or nothing, as M3.1b's shift is.
3. **This replaces the one-step shift.** `planShift`, `shift_blocked` and `carriedFrom.pushedOut` go, or are reshaped; say what replaced each. A missed day whose only move step is in order follows the reorder; its other steps keep their own actions (RULE-9 to RULE-12 for kinds that are not in order). MR-26 and MR-28 (2) still apply to steps that are not of an in-order move kind.
4. **After a break (MR-30 (1), ND-11).** The in-order steps on open-gap days join the queue like any other. Nothing else moves out of the break, and gap days keep `in_gap`.
5. **Not done this week (MR-30 (6)).** The steps left over at the end of the queue are counted in `weekCounts.toNextWeek` (replacing `pushedOut`; present only when the plan has `outcomes`). A missed day whose in-order step got no session this week gets a new outcome value (for example `next_week`) so M3.3 can show "next week starts with it". The body lists them in order per kind.
6. **Easy first hard session back (RULE-21, MR-31 (1)).** When there is an open gap, code puts exactly "First hard session after a few days off: keep the effort easy today." first in the instructions of the first hard step on the first open practice day this week after the gap that has a hard step. Once only (not again on a replan), the minutes unchanged, a guarded write, only when the switch is on and the carry switch allows writes.
7. **Warm-up line only on physical steps (MR-31 (2)).** MR-26's line goes on a moved or reordered step only when it is hard or the goal's profile template is `endurance` or `strength`. Never on lessons, writing, coding or other steps.
8. **Tests.** Generated weeks (extend `methodOrderGap.test.ts`) with missed days, breaks, lower-priority in-order steps, steps missed twice and replans check after every plan: no in-order step lost (each is done, in a later session in order, or listed as not done, in order); order kept; no day longer; nothing protected trimmed; the rest gap; nothing on the test day or a rest day; a replan changes nothing. Plus named tests: lessons 4 to 6 missed in a break, the rest of the week does 4, 5, 6 and 7 to 9 are listed for next week; a lower-priority lesson on a missed day is not lost; a moved lesson missed again is not lost; the last lesson of the week missed is listed for next week; a session too short for the next lesson gives up its own and the next one is tried; the easy line once, on the right step; the warm-up line on a strength step and not on a lesson; a reorder whose transaction loses one guard writes nothing; switch off, no profile, or untagged steps give exactly today's plan and body.

### Summary
With `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` on, a step of an in-order move kind (a kind with `inOrder` and action `move`: lessons, strength workouts) is never lost. With the switch off, `planRecovery` still returns `planCarries(input)` itself, and `planCarries` is unchanged.

- **The queue (requirement 1).** `inOrderQueue` in the new `backend/src/lib/recovery/inOrder.ts` (pure, exported for M3.1d). For every week and every in-order move kind, it lists the steps of the kind not done yet, in written order. First come the steps still on a day of the week (missed days, open days), then the ones already listed for next week. It reads only the stored week. A step's identity is the day it was written on plus its title. A step still on that day has no marker; a step placed elsewhere keeps that day in its `shiftedFrom`. A missed day is never rewritten, so after a move it still holds an old copy; the copy on the latest day is the live one. Each step gets a status: `missed`, `session` (open, today or later), `still_open` (a day before today that has not closed) or `next_week`. A step whose live copy is on a done day is done and is not listed.
- **The reorder (requirement 2).** For each queue, the week's remaining sessions of the kind (open, today or later, not the test day, holding the kind) keep their days. In date order, each session takes the next step of the queue: if it already holds that step, nothing changes; otherwise the step takes its own step's place (`takePlace`). The day never gets longer. A fixed, continue-marked, swapped, carried or in-order step is never trimmed. The rest gap holds as MR-29 (2) and (5) say. If a session can't take the next step safely, it gives its own step up (the day only gets shorter), that step stays in the queue in its place, and the next session is tried. Placed steps store `shiftedFrom: { taskId, date, replaced }`, where `taskId` and `date` now name the day the step was **written** on, however often it moves.
- **Next week (requirement 5).** The steps left at the end are listed in `reorders[].toNextWeek`, in order. The ones the reorder took off a session are stored nowhere else, so they are recorded as `toNextWeek: [{ taskId, date, step }]` on the queue's first missed step. A missed day row gets this one extra field and nothing else. Once listed, a step is never placed again that week, so a replan gives the same list. `weekCounts.toNextWeek` (present with `letGo`, `continued` and `noRoom`) counts them per week. A missed day whose in-order top step got no session has outcome and topStep `next_week`.
- **What it replaced (requirement 3).** `planShift`, `PlannedShift`, `ShiftedStep`, `carry.shifts`, the `shift_blocked` drop, `carriedFrom.pushedOut` and `weekCounts.pushedOut` are gone. They were replaced by the reorder, `PlannedReorder` (`carry.reorders`), outcome `next_week`, the `toNextWeek` records and `weekCounts.toNextWeek`. Ordinary carries now move a missed day's highest-priority move step that is **not** of an in-order move kind; MR-26 and MR-28 (2) apply only to those steps. In-order move steps are never left behind: a step already moved is still queued, and so is a lower-priority one.
- **After a break (requirement 4).** Gap days' in-order steps join the queue like any other. Nothing else is carried out of the break, and gap days keep `in_gap` even when their lesson found a session.
- **The easy line (requirement 6).** With an open gap, the first open practice day of today's week after the gap that has a hard step gets `EASY_START_LINE` first in its first hard step's instructions. The minutes don't change. It goes in `plan.easyStart` (the full new row) and is written with the same guard as the others. It is added once: if that step already starts with the line, nothing is planned. A step that a reorder moves drops the line, because the line belongs to the first day back.
- **The warm-up line (requirement 7).** `MOVED_WARM_UP_LINE` now goes on a moved or reordered step only when it is hard or the profile's template is `endurance` or `strength`. With both lines, the easy line comes first.
- **Writes.** `writeRecovery` writes every day of this run's reorders (and any carry, continue marker or easy line on those days, which share that day's final row) in one `prisma.$transaction`. Each day is guarded as before; a failed guard rolls all of them back. Other days keep one guarded write each. It returns `written`, `writtenContinues`, `writtenReorders` and `writtenEasyStart`, and the body adds the last two plus `reorders` and `easyStart` (switch on only).

### Differences from the docs
- RULE-20 says a swapped step is never replaced. As built, a session holding a swapped step makes its whole kind wait for the week until that day closes (`waits: 'swapped'`), and the same goes for a held key session (`held`). RULE-20 could name the waits.
- MR-29 (1), (3) and (6) are now history: there is no `pushedOut` and no "second missed step is no room", and `shiftedFrom` names the written day, not the session the step came from.
- `02-feature.md` section 7 could list `carry.reorders`, `easyStart`, `writtenReorders`, `writtenEasyStart`, the reshaped `shiftedFrom`, the `toNextWeek` records, outcome `next_week` and `weekCounts.toNextWeek`, and drop `shifts`, `pushedOut` and `shift_blocked`.
- RULE-15 / section 6: a gap day stays `in_gap` even when its lesson moved; the `next_week` line is for days outside a gap.
- `03-phases.md` M3.3 item (4) should say `carry.reorders`, `toNextWeek` and `next_week` instead of `carry.shifts`, `pushedOut` and `shift_blocked`.
