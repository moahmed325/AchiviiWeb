With the method switch off (no `outcomes`), the signals and `MissNotice` are exactly as today (test it). A day whose `rules` is `missed_sessions` keeps today's lines. (RULE-15, AC-7)
2. **When each line shows.** As missed sessions do: a line that names a receiving day (moved, moved in order, continue) shows until that receiving day closes, read from the stored markers (`carriedFrom`, `shiftedFrom`, `continueFrom`), not only from this run's plan, so it survives the next reconcile; every other line (let go, fixed, no room, next week, left out) shows only while its missed day is the most recent closed practice day. The order stays: gentle return, then the swap offer, then a line that names a receiving day, then the others; among days, the most recent missed day. `held` is the swap offer and `in_gap` the gentle return, as today. (RULE-15, MR-12, MR-28)
3. **Today's lines, exact.** Section 6's table, word for word: Moved (unchanged), Moved in order, Continue, Let go, Fixed, No room this week, In order to next week, Final week no room. "Moved, in order" is a `moved` day whose step now sits on a later session through a reorder (`shiftedFrom`); `{Weekday}` is that session's day. `{Day}` is "Yesterday" or a weekday. Into today, these exact variants: Moved keeps today's "…We moved its most important step into today, so today stays the same length."; Moved in order: "{Day}'s session didn't happen. We moved it into today, and the next ones follow on."; Continue: "{Day}'s session didn't happen. Today's session picks up where you left off." (AC-7)
4. **Set today aside.** When the day concerned is today, "{Day}'s session didn't happen." becomes "Today's session is set aside." and the rest of the line is the outcome's, for every outcome (missed sessions' two set-aside lines stay as they are). With nothing stored when nothing moves (ND-14 as built), the line shows from the action's answer and, as today, is gone after a reload; do not add storage. (section 6, M3.3 item 5)
5. **The Dashboard's lines, exact.** Section 6's Dashboard table. Into today: Moved keeps "{Day}'s most important step is part of today's session."; Continue: "{Day}'s work picks up again today." Set aside keeps "Today's session is set aside. No catching up needed." for every outcome. (AC-7)
6. **Choices from the server.** When `carry.choices` is present, Today's actions read it and nothing else: "Set today aside" only when `today.setAside`; "Swap with another day" only when `today.swapWith` has a day, listing exactly those days; on the held offer, "Just move its main step" only when that held day's `moveNowTaskId` is not null. When `choices` is absent (method switch off), `todayActions` and today's behavior stay exactly as they are (test it). The new 409 reasons (`fixed_step`, `out_of_order`, `rest_gap`, `holds_carry`, `holds_continue`, `would_not_move`) stay quiet failures. (RULE-16, AC-8, MR-34)
7. **Continue line.** A step with a `continueFrom` marker shows "Pick up where {Weekday} stopped." at the top of its instructions in Today and in Focus (both `FocusStepRunner` and `FocusSessionModal`), `{Weekday}` being the marker's date's weekday. Built on screen from the marker; the stored text is not changed. (RULE-10, section 6)
8. **Stored first lines on their own line.** In Today (steps and the 10-minute version) and both Focus views, a leading easy line and/or warm-up line shows as its own line(s) above the rest of the instructions, nothing else changes. (MR-26, MR-31, M3.3 item 3)
