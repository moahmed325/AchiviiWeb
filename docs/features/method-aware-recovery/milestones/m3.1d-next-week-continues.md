# M3.1d — Next week continues from where the person is

**Feature:** docs/features/method-aware-recovery/02-feature.md
**Branch:** mar-m3.1d-next-week-continues
**Prompt written:** 2026-10-11

## Prompt

### Context
M3.1c keeps in-order steps inside the week and lists the leftovers "for next week", but nothing that writes next week reads them yet. This milestone hands them on: code places them in the new week itself (MR-32), and the week writer is told where the person is. Everything here is behind `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` (off in production). Verify these facts; do not trust this prompt.

- Next week is written only from the weekly review: `routes/goal.ts` (`POST /weeks/:weekNumber/review`) calls `writeNextWeek(goal, weekNum, goal.dailyTasks, slotTime, recovery)` in `backend/src/lib/planV2.ts`, then `saveWeekTasks` (one transaction). `writeNextWeek` builds `lastWeek` (`LastWeekSummary` in `backend/src/lib/ai/weekPlan.ts`: target, result, done of planned, key sessions skipped) and calls `generateWeekPlan`; `lastWeekBlock` puts it in the prompt.
- The queue: `inOrderQueue({ days, tasks, profile, today })` and `inOrderMoveKinds` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/inOrder.ts` (M3.1c). A step's identity is the day it was written on plus its title; `shiftedFrom` names that day; leftovers taken off their session are stored as `toNextWeek` records on a step (any step; the queue reads records on every step).
- Placement helpers in `backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`: `takePlace`, `restGapHolds` (MR-29 (2) and (5)), `isPhysical` / `withWarmUpLine` (MR-31). RULE-21's easy line: `backDays` skips the test day. The method switch: `methodRecoveryEnabled()` in `backend/src/lib/reconcileCore.ts`. The final week: `TOTAL_WEEKS = 12` in `backend/src/lib/ai/roadmap.ts`.
- Tests that pin `writeNextWeek` today: `backend/test/writeNextWeekKeySessions.test.ts`, `writeNextWeekProfile.test.ts`, `weekPlan.test.ts` (prompt fixture), `weekPlanKinds.test.ts`.

### Goal
With the switch on, the new week's first sessions of each in-order move kind hold the steps the person has not done yet, placed by code, and the writer knows where the person is and how far behind. Nothing can make the week call fail that does not fail today. With the switch off, `writeNextWeek` is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **Switch off means exactly today (MR-32 (7)).** Every change below acts only when `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` is exactly `true`, read per call. Off: the week call gets exactly today's input and prompt, nothing is placed, and the saved week is exactly today's. A test runs the existing `writeNextWeek` fixtures with the switch off and compares the `generateWeekPlan` input and the returned days exactly with today's; the prompt fixture in `weekPlan.test.ts` does not change.
2. **What carries over.** At the week's end, for each in-order move kind, the steps not done yet in order (`inOrderQueue` on the finished week, records included). At a phase change they carry only if the kind is still in the goal's profile; otherwise they are let go and counted (MR-32 (4)). Old weeks without kinds carry nothing (RULE-18).
3. **Code places them (MR-32 (1), (2)).** After the week call returns, the new week's first sessions of that kind (practice days, not the test day, not rest), in date order, take the carried steps in order, each in place of the model's step of that kind, with `takePlace`'s fit (no day longer, nothing fixed, continue-marked or in-order of another kind trimmed) and the rest gap against last week's days (MR-29 (5)). A placed step keeps its identity (a `shiftedFrom` naming the day it was written on). A step that does not fit, or for which there is no session, waits: it is stored as a `toNextWeek` record inside the new week (on its first session of the kind, or on its first practice step when it has none), so the next handoff carries it again. Nothing is ever dropped here except at a phase change (requirement 2) or in the final week (requirement 6).
4. **The writer is told (MR-32 (1), (2), (3)).** A new block in the week prompt, only with the switch on and only when there is something to say: per in-order kind, the steps code will place (titles, in order), where the person is (the first step not done) and how many steps behind the plan that is (the number not done at the week's end), and "write any further step of this kind to continue after the last one listed"; the unfinished continue work (told, not placed); the moved steps of other kinds that found no room last week (from the stored week: a missed day outside the open gap whose top move step has no carry from it), marked hard or not. No new check on the answer: the existing RULE-6 checks are unchanged.
5. **No-room moves of other kinds (MR-32 (3)).** A non-hard one is placed by code in the new week's first session of its kind (in place of that session's step of the kind, same fit rules) when it fits; otherwise, and always for a hard one, it is only told.
6. **The final week (MR-32 (6)).** In week 12, nothing is listed for next week: M3.1c's leftovers in the final week get a new outcome (for example `left_out`, beside `next_week`) so M3.3 can show "This was the plan's last week, so it's left out", and `weekCounts.toNextWeek` is 0. No handoff is attempted after week 12.
7. **No double counting (logic check finding 9).** Once a step is handed on (placed or stored as a record in the new week), last week's queue no longer lists it: `inOrderQueue` ignores, in a week, any step whose identity also appears in a later week.
8. **RULE-21 on the test day (MR-32 (5)).** When the first day back after an open gap is the test day and its test step is hard, the easy line goes on the test step (once, as for any other step).
9. **Tests.** Switch off exactly today (requirement 1); lessons 4 to 9 not done and three lesson sessions next week: they hold 4, 5, 6, and 7 to 9 wait as records; the next handoff carries 7 to 9 first; a step too long for its session waits; no session of the kind: all wait; the prompt block lists what code places, the position and how far behind, and the no-room moves; a non-hard no-room move is placed, a hard one only told; a phase change with the kind kept carries, with the kind gone lets go; week 12 leftovers are `left_out`, `toNextWeek` 0; a handed-on step is gone from last week's list; the easy line on a hard test step after a break; the week call still fails and succeeds exactly as before (no new failure path). Generated tests: over many random finished weeks and handoffs, no in-order step is ever lost or placed out of order, no day is longer than written, and only phase changes and week 12 let a step go. The generated tests also count how often steps are waiting and the writer writes no session of their kind (so they wait another week), and how many weeks the longest wait lasts; report both numbers, so we know whether lessons could sit until week 12. This is a measurement only: no new failure path.

### Summary
With `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` exactly `true`, the weekly review's `writeNextWeek` reads what the finished week hands on, tells the week writer, and after the call code places the carried steps in the new week. With it off (or for a goal without a profile), the call gets exactly today's input and the week comes back as the same object.

- **What carries (req. 2).** New `backend/src/lib/recovery/handoff.ts`, `weekHandoff`: every practice day of the finished week that is not `completed` counts as not done (the week has ended). For each in-order move kind, `inOrderQueue` on that week (records included) gives the steps not done, in written order. A step whose kind is no longer an in-order move kind of the goal's profile goes to `letGo` (MR-32 (4)). Untagged weeks give nothing (RULE-18).
- **Code places them (req. 3).** `placeHandoff`: for each carried kind, the new week's steps of that kind on practice days (not test, not rest), in date then row order, take the carried steps in order with `takePlace` (no day longer; fixed, continue-marked, in-order and already-placed steps never trimmed) and the rest gap against the new week and last week's done days (MR-29 (2), (5)). A placed step gets `shiftedFrom: { taskId, date, replaced, stepNumber }` naming the day it was written on. A session that can't take the next step gives its own step up (MR-30 (5), as M3.1c), unless that is its day's only step: then the kind stops there. Steps left wait as `toNextWeek` records, in order, on the new week's first step of the kind (or its first practice step).
- **The writer is told (req. 4).** `handoffBrief` builds the lines; `WeekCallInput.whereTheyAre` adds a `WHERE THEY ARE` block at the end of the prompt, only when there are lines. Per in-order kind: the steps code places (titles, in order), where they are (the first), how far behind (the count), and "write any further step of this kind to continue after" the last one. Then unfinished continue work (a missed day's continue step after the kind's last done day), then no-room moves (a missed day outside any 3-day run, under the new rules, whose top move step that is not in order and not already moved has no carry from it), each marked hard or not. No new check on the answer.
- **No-room moves (req. 5).** A non-hard one takes the first session of its kind that code has not placed into, same fit rules; a hard one (by kind or `highLoad`) is only told.
- **Final week (req. 6).** In `planRecovery`, week 12's leftovers get outcome `left_out` (topStep too); the reorder has `toNextWeek: []` and a new `leftOut` list (only in that week); `weekCounts.toNextWeek` is 0. `writeNextWeek` already returns null after week 12 (tested).
- **No double counting (req. 7).** `inOrderQueue` ignores, in a week, any step whose identity also appears in a later week (live copy or record), and returns those keys as `handedOn` (only when any). In `planRecovery`, a week with handed-on steps keeps its sessions (`waits: 'handed_on'`), and its missed days keep outcome `next_week`.
- **RULE-21 on the test day (req. 8).** When the first open practice day back after the gap is the test day, its hard test step gets the easy line (once). A test day that is not the first day back is still skipped.
- **Records are never lost.** Records now live on session steps of the new week, which a later reorder or carry may replace, trim or give up. New `keepRecords` in `carry.ts` moves the records of any step a reorder or a method carry removes onto the step it places (or the first step kept). With no records stored (every week before M3.1d) it changes nothing.
- **Written order across weeks.** `inOrderQueue` now sorts all of a week's steps by written order (before: on-day steps, then listed ones), so steps from earlier weeks come first in the new week. `byWritten` reads `shiftedFrom.stepNumber` when present (M3.1d stores it), so two steps written the same day keep their order after a move.
- **The switch** moved to `recovery/switch.ts` so `planV2.ts` can read it without importing `reconcileCore.ts` (which imports `planV2.ts`); `reconcileCore.ts` re-exports it, same behavior.
- **No new failure.** Reading the handoff and placing it run inside try/catch in `writeNextWeek`: an error is logged and the week is written/kept as without the switch.

### Decisions I made
| Choice | Other option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| At the handoff, every practice day not `completed` counts as not done (status, not `classifyDays`). | Classify at the review time and leave open days out. | Next week is being written, so the week is over; an open day left out would never be handed on (lost). Risk noted under Problems. |
| A session that can't take the next carried step gives its own step up (unless it is the day's only step; then the kind stops and later sessions keep the model's steps). The model's step given up is dropped, like a replaced one. | Keep the model's step and try the next session; or stop at the first misfit. | MR-30 (5) and M3.1c's rule: order before keep going. The model's step there was written as "after the carried ones", so dropping it loses nothing written for the person yet. |
| Each step of the kind on a practice day is one session (two lessons on one day are two slots). | One session per day. | Uses what the writer wrote; the rest gap still forbids two hard steps on a day. |
| A kind no longer an in-order move kind of the profile is let go at any handoff, not only at a phase change; `letGo` is returned (counted) but not stored. | Only at a phase change; store the count. | The profile is per goal and cannot change today, so this never happens yet; a kind missing from the profile can never get a session, so keeping it would make it wait forever. Nothing reads the count yet, and storing it would need a place outside `detailedSteps`. |
| "Previous" profile = the stored one (or the one just made); the current one = the one the review passes. | One profile. | Lets the phase-change rule be tested and correct if profiles ever change per phase. |
| Placed and no-room-placed steps use `shiftedFrom` (with a new optional `stepNumber`). | A new marker (`handedOnFrom`). | `shiftedFrom` already means "placed from another day, keeps its written day"; every existing rule (never carried again, identity) then just works. No schema change. |
| A no-room step takes the first session of its kind that code has not already placed into; if that one doesn't fit, it is only told (no further search). | Search every session. | "First session of its kind" in MR-32 (3); one try keeps it simple. |
| Unfinished continue work covers every continue kind, not only in-order ones. | Only in-order continue kinds (RULE-20's sentence). | Requirement 4 lists "the unfinished continue work" without the limit; a drafting step is just as useful to tell. |
| Waiting records go on the new week's first step of the kind; `keepRecords` moves records when a reorder or method carry removes that step. | Anchor records on a step nothing ever replaces. | The prompt names the anchor; no such step exists outside the test day, which the queue does not read. |
