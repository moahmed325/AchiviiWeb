## Prompt

### Context
M3.1c keeps in-order steps inside the week and lists the leftovers "for next week", but nothing that writes next week reads them yet. This milestone hands them on: code places them in the new week itself (MR-32), and the week writer is told where the person is. Everything here is behind `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` (off in production). Verify these facts; do not trust this prompt.

- Next week is written only from the weekly review: `routes/goal.ts` (`POST /weeks/:weekNumber/review`) calls `writeNextWeek(goal, weekNum, goal.dailyTasks, slotTime, recovery)` in `backend/src/lib/planV2.ts`, then `saveWeekTasks` (one transaction). `writeNextWeek` builds `lastWeek` (`LastWeekSummary` in `backend/src/lib/ai/weekPlan.ts`: target, result, done of planned, key sessions skipped) and calls `generateWeekPlan`; `lastWeekBlock` puts it in the prompt.
- The queue: `inOrderQueue({ days, tasks, profile, today })` and `inOrderMoveKinds` in `backend/src/lib/recovery/inOrder.ts` (M3.1c). A step's identity is the day it was written on plus its title; `shiftedFrom` names that day; leftovers taken off their session are stored as `toNextWeek` records on a step (any step; the queue reads records on every step).
- Placement helpers in `backend/src/lib/recovery/carry.ts`: `takePlace`, `restGapHolds` (MR-29 (2) and (5)), `isPhysical` / `withWarmUpLine` (MR-31). RULE-21's easy line: `backDays` skips the test day. The method switch: `methodRecoveryEnabled()` in `backend/src/lib/reconcileCore.ts`. The final week: `TOTAL_WEEKS = 12` in `backend/src/lib/ai/roadmap.ts`.
- Tests that pin `writeNextWeek` today: `backend/test/writeNextWeekKeySessions.test.ts`, `writeNextWeekProfile.test.ts`, `weekPlan.test.ts` (prompt fixture), `weekPlanKinds.test.ts`.

### Goal
With the switch on, the new week's first sessions of each in-order move kind hold the steps the person has not done yet, placed by code, and the writer knows where the person is and how far behind. Nothing can make the week call fail that does not fail today. With the switch off, `writeNextWeek` is exactly as today.

### Requirements
1. **Switch off means exactly today (MR-32 (7)).** Every change below acts only when `METHOD_RECOVERY_ENABLED` is exactly `true`, read per call. Off: the week call gets exactly today's input and prompt, nothing is placed, and the saved week is exactly today's. A test runs the existing `writeNextWeek` fixtures with the switch off and compares the `generateWeekPlan` input and the returned days exactly with today's; the prompt fixture in `weekPlan.test.ts` does not change.
2. **What carries over.** At the week's end, for each in-order move kind, the steps not done yet in order (`inOrderQueue` on the finished week, records included). At a phase change they carry only if the kind is still in the goal's profile; otherwise they are let go and counted (MR-32 (4)). Old weeks without kinds carry nothing (RULE-18).
3. **Code places them (MR-32 (1), (2)).** After the week call returns, the new week's first sessions of that kind (practice days, not the test day, not rest), in date order, take the carried steps in order, each in place of the model's step of that kind, with `takePlace`'s fit (no day longer, nothing fixed, continue-marked or in-order of another kind trimmed) and the rest gap against last week's days (MR-29 (5)). A placed step keeps its identity (a `shiftedFrom` naming the day it was written on). A step that does not fit, or for which there is no session, waits: it is stored as a `toNextWeek` record inside the new week (on its first session of the kind, or on its first practice step when it has none), so the next handoff carries it again. Nothing is ever dropped here except at a phase change (requirement 2) or in the final week (requirement 6).
4. **The writer is told (MR-32 (1), (2), (3)).** A new block in the week prompt, only with the switch on and only when there is something to say: per in-order kind, the steps code will place (titles, in order), where the person is (the first step not done) and how many steps behind the plan that is (the number not done at the week's end), and "write any further step of this kind to continue after the last one listed"; the unfinished continue work (told, not placed); the moved steps of other kinds that found no room last week (from the stored week: a missed day outside the open gap whose top move step has no carry from it), marked hard or not. No new check on the answer: the existing RULE-6 checks are unchanged.
5. **No-room moves of other kinds (MR-32 (3)).** A non-hard one is placed by code in the new week's first session of its kind (in place of that session's step of the kind, same fit rules) when it fits; otherwise, and always for a hard one, it is only told.
6. **The final week (MR-32 (6)).** In week 12, nothing is listed for next week: M3.1c's leftovers in the final week get a new outcome (for example `left_out`, beside `next_week`) so M3.3 can show "This was the plan's last week, so it's left out", and `weekCounts.toNextWeek` is 0. No handoff is attempted after week 12.
7. **No double counting (logic check finding 9).** Once a step is handed on (placed or stored as a record in the new week), last week's queue no longer lists it: `inOrderQueue` ignores, in a week, any step whose identity also appears in a later week.
8. **RULE-21 on the test day (MR-32 (5)).** When the first day back after an open gap is the test day and its test step is hard, the easy line goes on the test step (once, as for any other step).
9. **Tests.** Switch off exactly today (requirement 1); lessons 4 to 9 not done and three lesson sessions next week: they hold 4, 5, 6, and 7 to 9 wait as records; the next handoff carries 7 to 9 first; a step too long for its session waits; no session of the kind: all wait; the prompt block lists what code places, the position and how far behind, and the no-room moves; a non-hard no-room move is placed, a hard one only told; a phase change with the kind kept carries, with the kind gone lets go; week 12 leftovers are `left_out`, `toNextWeek` 0; a handed-on step is gone from last week's list; the easy line on a hard test step after a break; the week call still fails and succeeds exactly as before (no new failure path). Generated tests: over many random finished weeks and handoffs, no in-order step is ever lost or placed out of order, no day is longer than written, and only phase changes and week 12 let a step go. The generated tests also count how often steps are waiting and the writer writes no session of their kind (so they wait another week), and how many weeks the longest wait lasts; report both numbers, so we know whether lessons could sit until week 12. This is a measurement only: no new failure path.
